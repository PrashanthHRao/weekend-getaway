Food & Drink

  • 🌱 Strandhill Market Kitchen — artisan coffee, burritos, pastries right by the beach
  • 🌱 Flip Side, Sligo Town — specialty coffee, brunch, toasties, baby-friendly
  • 🌱 Katie's Kitchen, Sligo Town — homemade soups, sandwiches, cakes
  • 🍴 The Vintage Kitchen, Sligo — Irish tapas with veggie options, craft beers
  • 🌱 The Blueberry Cafe, Carrick — fresh scones, soup, coffee near Slieve League
  • 🍴 The Rusty Mackerel, Carrick — award-winning seafood, relaxed atmosphere

Accommodation

  • Mountain View, Grange Beg (Sleeps 5) — 3-bed near Benbulben
  • Mountain View (Sleeps 6) — same location, larger option
  • Kit's Cottage, Carrick (Sleeps 5) — near Slieve League

Tips

  • Longest drives of all plans — worth it for the scenery
  • Slieve League is free and less crowded than Moher
  • Benbulben viewpoint is a quick roadside stop on N15
  • Pack layers — Atlantic coast can be windy

🍴

Talbot's Bar & Restaurant or The Dressing Room Cafe. Fresh local seafood, pub classics.
20 min from Downpatrick
14:30
⛵
Drive to Achill Island
Cross Michael Davitt Bridge to Achill. Scenic coastal road with Atlantic views.
45 min via R313
15:30
📷
Achill Island Coastal Drive
Minaun Cliffs viewpoint, Keel Lake, Dooega Beach. Wild Atlantic scenery. Photo stops along the way.
17:00
🏠
Check in — Sound Cottage, Achill Island
3-bed detached cottage, quiet location. Sleeps 6. Peaceful countryside near the sound.
🔗 View & Book (Selfcater)
🌊 Day 2 — Keem Bay & Return
09:00
☕
Breakfast at The Achill Bakery Cafe Cafe
Freshly baked scones, sourdough, good coffee. Family-run spot.
10:00
🌴
Keem Bay
Stunning Blue Flag beach in a horseshoe bay. Crystal-clear water, white sand. One of Ireland's most beautiful beaches. Paddle on calm days.
20 min from cottage
12:00
📷
Saddle Head & Deserted Village
Ruined village at the western tip of Achill. Eerie, beautiful, great photos. 30 min.
15 min from Keem
13:00
🍴
Lunch in Westport Cafe
The Creel or An Port Mor. Vibrant town with great food scene.
40 min via N59
14:30
🚶
Westport Town Stroll
The Octagon, Riverwalk, ice cream stop. Compact & charming Georgian town.
15:30
🚗
Return to Dublin
N5/N4/M4 east (3hr). Home ~18:30.

Food & Drink

  • 🌱 The Achill Bakery Cafe, Keel — artisan bakery, great coffee, breakfast baps
  • 🍴 Talbot's Bar, Belmullet — pub food with sea views, baby-friendly corner
  • 🌱 The Dressing Room Cafe, Bangor Erris — carvery, salad bar, homemade desserts
  • 🍴 The Beehive Cafe, Westport — tapas & pizza, relaxed vibe
  • 🌱 An Port Mor, Westport — cafe by day, bistro by night, great scones
  • 🍴 The Creel, Westport — fresh seafood, veggie bowls, baby-friendly

Accommodation

  • Sound Cottage, Achill Island — 3-bed, sleeps 6. Quiet countryside location.
  • 3-Bed in Achill Island — stylish home with Clew Bay & Minaun Cliffs views.
  • Glenvale Cottage — 3-bed in Dugort, near Dooega Beach.

Tips

  • Ceide Fields visitor centre is closed in winter — check opening times for May
  • Keem Bay is Ireland's most photographed beach — visit early for fewer crowds
  • Achill Island is the largest island off Ireland — lots to explore
  • Downpatrick Head has a blowhole that sprays on windy days
  • All stops in Republic of Ireland — no passport needed
